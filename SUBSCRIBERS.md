# GNS+ Subscriber Log

Every new GNS+ account starts a 2-week free trial. When the trial ends, the assigned GNS representative
circles back with the subscriber to go over their contract and pick a plan: GNS+ Regular (ads), GNS+ Gold (no ads)
or GNS+ Premium (no ads, exclusives, backstage content and fan perks).

Representatives are assigned at random when an account is created, keeping the load even:
Grace Langley, Glenn Bourbon, Henry Reyes, Peyton Manning, Joseph Reyes-McCollum.

This log is mirrored in `SUBSCRIBERS` in `index.html`, which the site uses for each subscriber's trial dates and plan.
